Open in any mobile browser, add to your home screen, and you're done. 01 OPEN THE PORTAL Visit the portal URL on your phone. It installs like an app — tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari or Chrome. You'll see the Co.13 icon on your home screen. No App Store, no account creation. 02 LOG IN WITH YOUR PIN Select your name from the roster and enter your 4-digit PIN. Your account is pre-created by the admin — no passwords, no emails, no friction. 03 CLOCK IN WHEN YOU ARRIVE Tap the big clock on the Clock tab. Select your duty type. That's it — your LOSAP time starts. The portal reminds you at 3.5 hours if you forget to clock out. 04 SIGN UP FOR STAFFING Head to the Staffing tab. See open positions on Engine 131, Truck 13, and all apparatus. Tap Sign Up, pick your shift window, done. Rank requirements are enforced automatically. 05 WATCH YOUR POINTS GROW Every duty hour, meeting, drill, training, and response is tracked and totaled on your LOSAP tab. See exactly where you stand toward the 50-point qualification threshold. AACVFA §3 COMPLIANCE EVERY POINT. AUTOMATIC.
