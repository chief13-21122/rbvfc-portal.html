This is a Demo — We Are in the Building, Research & Development Phase

What you are about to see is a working prototype, not a finished product. The portal you will explore is fully functional and already tracking real LOSAP data — but it is actively being tested, refined, and improved before a full company-wide rollout.

We are testing real workflows with real data to find what works, what doesn't, and what needs to be added before this becomes the official system every member relies on.

Rushing software into a fire station creates risk. We are doing this right — identifying edge cases, hardening the logic, and validating every LOSAP calculation against AACVFA §3 before it counts.

🚀 Where We're Headed

The goal is a system the whole company trusts and uses daily — for staffing, points, responses, training, and communication. This demo shows how close we already are.

Your feedback during this phase directly shapes the final product. If something doesn't feel right or could work better — that's exactly the kind of input we need right now.

Getting Started

Simple For
Every Member

No app to download. Open in any mobile browser, add to your home screen, and you're done.

01

Open the Portal

Visit the portal URL on your phone. It installs like an app — tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari or Chrome. You'll see the Co.13 icon on your home screen. No App Store, no account creation.

02

Log In With Your PIN

Select your name from the roster and enter your 4-digit PIN. Your account is pre-created by the admin — no passwords, no emails, no friction.

03

Clock In When You Arrive

Tap the big clock on the Clock tab. Select your duty type. That's it — your LOSAP time starts. The portal reminds you at 3.5 hours if you forget to clock out.

04

Sign Up For Staffing

Head to the Staffing tab. See open positions on Engine 131, Truck 13, and all apparatus. Tap Sign Up, pick your shift window, done. Rank requirements are enforced automatically.

05

Watch Your Points Grow

Every duty hour, meeting, drill, training, and response is tracked and totaled on your LOSAP tab. See exactly where you stand toward the 50-point qualification threshold.

Always In The Loop

Push Notifications
That Matter

Real-time alerts delivered straight to your phone — no app open required. Members stay informed automatically.

Staffing Alerts

Officers can blast custom alerts to all members or specific alert groups when apparatus needs to be staffed.

Duty Check-In

At the 3.5-hour mark, a push notification asks "Still on duty?" Confirm or clock out directly from the notification.

Auto Clock-Out Alert

If a check-in goes unacknowledged for 30 minutes, the member is clocked out automatically and receives a push confirming the time recorded.

LOSAP Approvals

When a member submits training or an activity for another member, the approving officer receives an instant push notification.
