Getting Started

SIMPLE FOR
EVERY MEMBER

No app to download. Works on any phone, any browser.

01
Open the Portal

Visit the portal URL on your phone. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen — it installs like an app, no download needed.

02
Log In With Your PIN

Select your name from the roster and enter your 4-digit PIN. Account is pre-created — no passwords, no email.

03
Clock In When You Arrive

Tap the clock on the Clock tab, select your duty type. LOSAP time starts. You'll get a reminder if you forget to clock out.

Common Questions

A WEBSITE.
NOT AN APP.

Good question — and one you'll probably hear from other members. Here's the real answer.

Short answer: This website is the app. The portal is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — it installs on your home screen and works exactly like a native app, without any app store.
📱
Installs on Your Home Screen

Tap Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or Menu → Add on Android. Launches full-screen, feels like an app.

🚫
No Downloads or Updates

No App Store, no Google Play, no waiting. When we push an update it's live for everyone automatically.

🌐
Works on Any Device

iPhone, Android, tablet, or the station computer — same experience everywhere.

⚡
Faster to Build & Fix

If something breaks before a drill, we can fix a web app the same night — not after a 3-day App Store review.

🔒
Just as Secure

PIN authentication, member-only access, HTTPS encryption — same standards used by banks.

🔧
Built Around Your Feedback

Tell us something needs to change, we can change it the same day — no App Store review cycle.
